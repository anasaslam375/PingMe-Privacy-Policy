## Overview

PingMe is an Android anti-theft and device-protection toolkit for a phone you own or are authorized to manage. It processes data on your device to respond to SMS commands from phone numbers you authorize. This policy explains what is handled and how it stays under your control.

PingMe is built for lawful personal or organizational anti-theft protection and recovery aid — not for secretly monitoring someone else’s phone without lawful authority and consent.

## Who can control this device

Only phone numbers you add under **Numbers** can trigger commands. Messages from any other number are ignored.

## Data processed on-device

- SMS command text from authorized numbers
- Device, network, and GPS details for Where, THEFT, SOS, and low-battery location / status replies
- Photos, screenshots, audio, screen, and camera videos you trigger (ASP, BASP, SS, REC, SREC, VREC / VFREC / VBREC, and SOS / THEFT evidence) — stored locally in Captures
- App usage summaries for the CAL command (when Usage access is granted)
- Lock / SNP / THEFT state while those features are enabled
- Authorized numbers, Command PIN, app-lock preferences, and other settings you configure

## Captures are not shared

Capture files (photos, screenshots, mic audio, screen video, camera video) remain on this device in PingMe’s private Captures storage. PingMe does not upload them to a PingMe server, does not send them as MMS or email attachments, and does not offer a share/export action from Captures. You can preview or delete them on this device.

## Where data is stored

Captures and related files are stored locally on the device. Authorized numbers and preferences are saved in app storage on the device. PingMe does not operate a PingMe cloud account for these features.

## What leaves the device

Command replies are plain-text SMS messages sent only to the authorized number that issued the command. Those replies may include:

- Location / maps links and device or network snapshot text
- Command status (for example, that a capture was saved)

Capture success replies confirm that a file was saved in Captures — they do **not** include media bytes or full filesystem paths.

SMS delivery uses your mobile carrier. Standard SMS is not end-to-end encrypted by PingMe.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Capture media stays on the device. SMS replies are delivered by your carrier only to the contact you authorized. We do not share Captures or authorized-number lists with third-party analytics or advertising partners.

## Permissions

PingMe requests permissions only to power anti-theft and activity commands you enable. Each sensitive permission is explained in-app before you grant it. You can revoke permissions in Android settings at any time.

| Permission | Why PingMe may need it |
|---|---|
| SMS | Receive commands from authorized numbers and send plain-text status / location replies |
| Phone | CALL-back replies and SIM / network identity for anti-theft alerts |
| Location (including background / Allow all the time) | Where, THEFT, SOS, and low-battery alerts when the app may be closed |
| Camera | ASP / BASP photos, SOS / THEFT evidence, and VREC camera video — saved in Captures |
| Microphone | REC / CREC audio and VREC video audio — saved in Captures |
| Notifications | Ongoing status for SNP, RING, THEFT, and capture services (Android 13+) |
| Device Admin | Lock and THEFT screen lock; SNP unlock policies |
| Accessibility | SNP panel lock, SS screenshots, THEFT volume-key block, lock-screen CALL controls |
| Notification access | Hide interrupting alerts while SNP is on |
| Do Not Disturb | Silence interruptions during SNP |
| Usage access | CAL app-usage summary replies |
| Battery unrestricted | Keep the SMS listener alive in the background |
| Screen capture | SREC screen recording after you approve Android’s cast prompt |
| Full-screen intent | Show the SREC Allow dialog when the app is in the background or the phone is locked (Android 14+) |

Related system capabilities (boot restart, wake lock, vibrate, audio settings) support RING / THEFT and keeping the SMS listener alive. Biometrics are used only for optional app lock.

Remote camera, microphone, and screen capture can run after an authorized SMS command and may show an ongoing notification while active. Media stays on-device.

## Security controls

You can set a Command PIN (required for Lock, CALL, captures, and SNP when enabled), enable app lock for Captures / Numbers / Activity, use Minimal Where replies, and auto-delete old captures. SIM changes can alert your authorized numbers.

## Your choices / retention & deletion

You can:

- Remove authorized numbers
- Delete captures in Captures
- Disable individual commands
- Revoke permissions in Android settings
- Clear app storage or uninstall PingMe

Doing so stops further processing for those features.
