## Overview

PingMe is published by AS-Tech Studios. It is an Android anti-theft and device-protection toolkit for a device you own or are authorized to manage. It processes data on your device to respond to SMS commands from phone numbers you authorize. This policy explains what is handled and how it stays under your control.

## Data processed on-device

- SMS command text from authorized numbers
- Device, network, and GPS details for location / status replies
- Photos, screenshots, audio, screen, and camera videos you trigger — stored locally in Captures
- App usage summaries for the CAL command (when Usage access is granted)
- Lock / SNP state while those features are enabled
- Authorized numbers, PIN, and preference settings you configure

## What leaves the device

Command replies are plain-text SMS messages sent only to the authorized number that issued the command. Replies may include location links, device/network info, and command status. Capture success replies confirm that a file was saved locally — they do not include media bytes.

PingMe does not operate a PingMe cloud account for Captures and does not upload capture files to AS-Tech Studios servers.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Capture media stays on the device. SMS replies are delivered by your mobile carrier to the contact you authorized.

## Permissions

PingMe may request SMS, phone, location (including background location), camera, microphone, notifications, device admin, accessibility, usage access, and related capabilities only to power anti-theft and activity commands you enable. You can revoke permissions in Android settings at any time.

## Retention & deletion

You can delete captures in-app, remove authorized numbers, clear app storage, or uninstall PingMe to stop further processing.

## Children

PingMe is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when features change. Material updates may also appear in the in-app privacy notice and require acceptance again.
